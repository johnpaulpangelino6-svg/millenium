## 🚦 IF THINGS GO WRONG

### Demo Computer Crashes:
**Backup:** Show screenshots in presentation, explain features verbally

### Internet Connection Lost:
**Backup:** System works offline with cached data, use local demo

### Technical Question You Can't Answer:
**Response:** "Great question. Let me get you the detailed specs after this meeting."

### Time Running Short:
**Priority Slides:** 1, 4, 8, 14, 16, 24 (intro, problem, demo, value, progress, action)

### Tough Question About Costs:
**Redirect:** "The ROI is 129%. Even if costs are 20% higher, we're still saving ₱700K+ per year."
