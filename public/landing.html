Features

One system for the whole lifecycle of a board

From the moment a board is registered to the day it is retired, every record, repair and report lives in the same place.

Executive dashboard

Fleet health, open tickets, low stock and recent activity at a glance — so you know what needs attention before it becomes a problem.

Admin

Device fleet management

Register each board, track serial numbers, OS and OPS module, location and live status. Search by ID, client, serial or room.

All roles

Maintenance ticketing

Report a fault, attach it to the right board, assign a technician and follow the repair through every stage to resolution.

All roles

Warranty tracking

Know instantly whether a board is still covered. Scan the sticker on the unit and the system reports the remaining cover.

Admin

Inventory & spare parts

Track stock levels for panels, OPS modules and accessories, with automatic low-stock and out-of-stock alerts.

Admin · Technician

Predictive maintenance

Early-warning signals on units showing rising failure risk, so parts can be ordered and repairs scheduled before a board goes down.

Admin

Analytics & reports

Business and service reporting — repair turnaround, recurring faults, stock consumption and per-client activity.

Admin

Content management

Publish and schedule the playlists, lessons and welcome screens that run on the boards across your sites.

Admin

Rentals & scheduling

Book a board for a date range, avoid double-booking, and let customers see and manage their own reservations.

Admin · Customer

QR & barcode scanning

Scan a board's label with your camera or upload a photo of it to pull up the exact unit, its warranty and its history.

All roles

Customer self-service

A portal where clients see only their own boards, raise their own tickets and chat directly with the service team.

How it works

From sign-in to a resolved repair

The normal day-to-day flow, in the order you will actually use it. Skip to whichever step you need.

01

Sign in to your account

Use the username and password issued to you. What you see afterwards depends on your role — the system loads only the modules you are allowed to use.

Login screenAny role
02

Find the board you care about

Open Devices and search by device ID, client, serial number or room. You get its live status, OS version, OPS module and last-seen time.

DevicesAny role
03

Raise a ticket when something breaks

Open a ticket against the affected board, describe the fault and set a priority. Customers can do this from their own portal.

TicketsAny role
04

Track the repair to completion

Technicians pick up assigned jobs and move them through Received → Diagnosing → Repairing → Resolved. Parts come straight from Inventory.

TicketsInventoryAdmin · Technician

Keep an eye on the bigger picture

Use the dashboard, predictive alerts and reports to spot repeat offenders, watch stock, and check whether a board is still under warranty.

Your role decides which modules appear in the sidebar. If a module is not listed below, it will not be visible to that role at all.

Administrator

Full system access

  • See every board, ticket and client
  • Manage users, customers and allocations
  • Register and retire devices
  • Track warranty and inventory

  • Review analytics, reports and audit logs
  • Approve and schedule rentals

About

Built around the Millennium SmartBoard

Millennium is a dynamic interactive touch panel used for presentations in classrooms, conference rooms and home theatres — anywhere that calls for collaborative, engaging content. This management system is the operations layer behind those boards.

It was built by Brains Infinite Innovations Inc. so that schools, offices and service teams could stop tracking boards in spreadsheets. Instead, every unit has a permanent record: where it is, who owns it, what has been replaced on it, whether it is still under warranty, and when it is due for attention.

  • One shared record for every board across all your sites.
  • Role-based access — people see only what their job needs.
  • Faults caught early, so fewer lessons and meetings are interrupted.
  • Cloud-hosted, so it works from a phone in the corridor or a desk.
