Getting started

How do I sign in?
  1. Open the portal and pick your role: Admin, Technician or Customer.
  2. Enter your email address and password.
  3. Select Sign In. You land on the dashboard for your role.

No account yet? Use Register under the form, or press Fill Admin credentials to look around with the demo account.

What can each role see?
  • Admin — everything: devices, tickets, inventory, warranty, rentals, users and reports.
  • Technician — the boards assigned to them, their job queue and the ticket history.
  • Customer — only their own displays, bookings and tickets. Other customers are never visible.
Where do I start if I am new?
  1. Check the dashboard — the four cards show your fleet, what is online, what needs attention and open tickets.
  2. Open Devices to see every SmartBoard and its status.
  3. Anything flagged Needs attention is also listed under Tickets.

SmartBoards & devices

How do I register a new SmartBoard?
  1. Go to Devices.
  2. Select Register a SmartBoard.
  3. Choose the model, the client type (school or company), then the customer, room and city.
  4. Select Save. The board appears at the top of the list with a generated device ID.
How do I hand a board over to a customer?

Open the device row and edit it, then set Customer & site. While a board has no customer it stays under Unassigned and cannot be booked for rent.

A board says Offline — what do I check?
  1. Confirm the board is powered on and awake.
  2. Check it is connected to the same network as before — a changed Wi-Fi or a new router is the usual cause.
  3. Open Devices and read the last-seen time on the device card.
  4. If it has been offline for more than a day, raise a ticket so it is tracked.

A board that has never checked in will show Offline from the moment it is registered — that is expected until its first connection.

How do I print the QR label for a board?

Use the QR action on the device row. The label carries the device ID, so scanning it opens the same record — useful for stock takes and for field technicians.

Service tickets

How do I raise a service ticket?
  1. Open Tickets and select New Ticket.
  2. Pick the device, describe the fault and set the priority.
  3. Submit. The ticket starts as Just reported.
How do I move a ticket forward?

Open the ticket and use the action button. The flow is:

  1. Just reported → Diagnosing
  2. Diagnosing → Repairing
  3. Repairing → Resolved

Each step records who changed it and when, so the history stays accurate.

Who can see the ticket I raised?

Admins see every ticket. Technicians see the ones assigned to them. Customers see only their own — tickets are filtered by account, never by what is on screen.

Rentals & bookings

How do I book a device for rent?
  1. Open Rentals and select New Booking.
  2. Choose the device, the customer, and the pickup and return dates.
  3. Add the delivery address if it is being shipped, then save.

The booking starts as Pending until an admin approves it.

How does approval work?
  1. An admin opens the booking and selects Approve.
  2. At hand-over, Activate starts the rental period.
  3. When the unit comes back, mark it Returned to close the booking.
A rental is past its return date

Overdue bookings are flagged automatically and counted on the Overdue card. Open the booking to see the customer and contact details, then either extend the dates or mark it overdue so it stays visible in the queue.

Parts & stock

How do I add a new part?
  1. Open Inventory.
  2. Select New Part Catalog and fill in the name, category and price.
  3. Set the minimum threshold — that is the level at which the part is flagged for reorder.
How do I add or deduct stock?

Use Add Stock on the part to record a delivery, or Deduct when a part is used on a repair. Both actions keep a running quantity, so the count always matches what was booked in and out.

Account & access

How do I change my password?
  1. Open Settings at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Go to the security section and enter your current password.
  3. Type the new password twice and confirm.
Why was I signed out on my own?

Sessions expire after a period of inactivity, and signing in on another device can end the older one. Simply sign in again — nothing is lost, because every change is saved as you make it.

Tick Remember me for 30 days on the sign-in screen to stay signed in on a device you trust.

Can I change how the system looks?

Yes — Settings → Appearance switches between light and dark, and changes density and text size. The choice is remembered on this device.

Something is wrong

I cannot sign in
  1. Check the role tab matches your account — an admin account will not sign in from the Customer tab.
  2. Re-type the password; the field is case sensitive.
  3. Try Forgot Password? to reset it by email.
  4. Still locked out? Email support and include the address you are using.
A page is blank, stuck, or shows old data
  1. Refresh the page once — a stale session is the usual cause.
  2. If it stays blank, sign out and back in.
  3. Clear the browser cache if it happens repeatedly.
  4. If it still fails, note the page name and the time, then contact support.
My change did not save

A change only saves when the form closes successfully. If the dialog stayed open or showed an error, nothing was written — reopen it and submit again. If it keeps failing, the session may have expired: refresh, sign in, and retry.

How do I report a bug or ask for a change?

Email support with three things: what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps. Include the device ID or ticket number if the problem involves a specific record.
